---
title: "The causal gap"
summary: "Most analytics questions are causal. Most analytics methods are only descriptive."
date: 2026-09-18
tag: causal-inference
---

Eighteen years across data, operations, finance, and sales made the gap
impossible to ignore. Decision-makers ask what will happen if they act; most
dashboards answer what already happened. The methods that close that gap,
experiments and observational designs, are the ones worth learning first.
