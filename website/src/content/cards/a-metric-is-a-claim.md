---
title: "A metric is a claim"
summary: "Every metric definition is a claim that needs a story behind it."
date: 2026-09-18
tag: semantic-layer
---

A number on a dashboard is a compressed sentence. "Revenue" only means
something once you fix the filters, the time window, and the exclusions.
The semantic layer is where those decisions get made once and reused by
people, notebooks, and AI agents drawing from the same source.
